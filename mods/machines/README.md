# Assorted Machines

Adds the alloy forge and the grinding mill, each in four tiers that work faster as you upgrade them. The grinding mill turns ores into two dusts and the alloy forge melts dusts together into bronze, electrum, invar and steel. It also adds dusts and gears for iron, gold, copper and the alloys.

The metals to build and feed the machines come from [Assorted Ores](../ores) or any other mod that adds them.

Also included in [Assorted Core](../core). Worlds made with Assorted Core 10.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
