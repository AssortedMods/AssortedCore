# Assorted Ores

Adds six new metals and four gems to the world. Tin, silver, aluminum, nickel, platinum and lead each come with a stone and deepslate ore, raw ore, ingots, nuggets, a dust, a gear and storage blocks. Ruby, peridot, sapphire and topaz drop straight from their ores and pack into blocks. The other Assorted mods and anything else that reads the common tags can use them.

With [Assorted Machines](../machines) installed the ores also grind into dusts and the gem ores into two gems.

Also included in [Assorted Core](../core). Worlds made with Assorted Core 10.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
